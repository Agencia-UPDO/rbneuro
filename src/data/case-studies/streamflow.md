### Challenge:

StreamFlow had a large content library but weak discovery and metadata. Users couldn't find what matched their interests, leading to low engagement and rising churn.

### Solution:

Nexsas delivered an AI content tagging and discovery pipeline that auto-tagged catalog items and powered search, browse, and "because you watched" experiences. Recommendations and surfacing were updated continuously from viewing and engagement data.

### Results:

- **78% Better content discovery:** Users found relevant content more often via improved search and recommendations.
- **41% Lower churn:** Higher engagement and satisfaction reduced cancellation and drop-off.
- **2.5x Engagement lift:** Watch time and sessions per user increased significantly.

### What They Said:

> "Content discovery used to be our biggest weakness. Nexsas made it a strength and our engagement metrics prove it." – StreamFlow
