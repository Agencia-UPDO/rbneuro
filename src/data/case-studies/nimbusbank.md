### Challenge:

NimbusBank needed to grow its customer base without increasing fraud risk or diluting quality. Legacy marketing and manual lead scoring couldn't keep up with volume or sophistication of attacks.

### Solution:

Nexsas deployed an AI-driven campaign and lead-scoring stack that unified first-party data, intent signals, and fraud indicators. Campaigns were optimized in real time for qualified leads while blocking bad actors before they reached the funnel.

### Results:

- **72% More qualified leads:** Targeting and scoring improvements doubled down on high-intent, low-fraud prospects.
- **22% Drop in fraud:** ML-based fraud detection reduced fake signups and chargebacks.
- **8.2x LTV growth YoY:** Better lead quality and onboarding flows drove significantly higher lifetime value.
