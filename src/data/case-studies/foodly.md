### Challenge:

Foodly's delivery network was struggling with late orders, failed deliveries, and low conversion rates. Manual dispatch and routing couldn't scale with demand, leading to customer churn and rising operational costs.

### Solution:

Nexsas implemented an AI-powered delivery optimization platform that unified routing, demand forecasting, and real-time scheduling. Machine learning models predicted demand by zone and time, while smart dispatch reduced empty miles and improved driver utilization.

### Results:

- **85% More conversions:** Friction-free booking and accurate ETAs increased completed orders.
- **34% Failed deliveries:** Fewer missed or late deliveries through smarter routing and capacity planning.
- **74% More successful deliveries:** First-attempt success improved with predictive timing and customer availability.
- **4x Faster scheduling:** Automated slot assignment and rebalancing cut scheduling time dramatically.
