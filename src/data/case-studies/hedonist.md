### Challenge:

Hedonist offered rich travel and experience inventory, but guests often felt overwhelmed. Manual curation and slow search led to lower satisfaction and fewer repeat bookings.

### Solution:

Nexsas implemented a predictive curation and discovery layer that combined preference learning, availability, and context. Guests received tailored suggestions and dynamic packages, with results ranked in real time.

### Results:

- **92% CSAT:** Guests reported much higher satisfaction with trip and experience choices.
- **22% More repeat bookings:** Personalized follow-up and "next trip" ideas drove repeat behavior.
- **3x Faster recommendations:** Instant, relevant results replaced long browsing and support calls.

### What They Said:

> "Our guests now find what they love in seconds. Nexsas turned our catalog into a concierge." – Hedonist
