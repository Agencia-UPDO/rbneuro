### Challenge:

TechVentures' sales team was drowning in leads but missing the best opportunities. Manual scoring and slow follow-up meant hot leads went cold and pipeline predictability was poor.

### Solution:

Nexsas built an AI lead-scoring and outreach system that ranked leads by fit and behavior, triggered timely touchpoints, and suggested next best actions. CRM and email were integrated so reps could act quickly on the right prospects.

### Results:

- **95% Lead accuracy:** Scoring and routing ensured the best leads reached the right reps first.
- **28% More pipeline:** Higher conversion from lead to opportunity and deal through speed and relevance.
- **5x Faster response time:** Automated alerts and templates cut time-to-first-touch and improved close rates.

> "We respond to our best leads in minutes, not days. Nexsas put our pipeline on autopilot without losing the human touch." – TechVentures
