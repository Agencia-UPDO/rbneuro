### Challenge:

BrightNest's product catalog was large and diverse; customers struggled to find the right fit. Generic recommendations and one-size-fits-all messaging led to low repeat rates and stagnant revenue.

### Solution:

Nexsas built a personalized recommendation and messaging engine powered by collaborative filtering and behavioral signals. Product discovery, email, and on-site content were tailored to each user's taste and lifecycle stage.

### Results:

- **120% Revenue growth:** Higher AOV and conversion from relevant recommendations and offers.
- **64% More repeat buyers:** Re-engagement and next-best-action flows brought customers back more often.
