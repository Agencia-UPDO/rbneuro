### Challenge:

GreenShift faced excess inventory, stockouts, and thin margins. Manual planning and spreadsheets couldn't handle seasonality or demand shifts, leading to waste and missed revenue.

### Solution:

Nexsas implemented a predictive inventory and demand-forecasting engine that used historical data, seasonality, and external signals. Replenishment and allocation were automated to minimize waste and maximize availability.

### Results:

- **67% Less waste:** Right-sized inventory and better expiry management cut write-offs and disposal.
- **19% Margin improvement:** Fewer markdowns and better sell-through improved unit economics.
- **4x Forecast accuracy:** Demand predictions improved dramatically, enabling confident planning and purchasing.
