## Email & SMS marketing

Unlock the true potential of your audience with targeted email and SMS campaigns. Our Email & SMS marketing service helps you reach the right people at the right time with personalized messages that drive opens, clicks, and conversions. Using segmentation, automation, and performance analytics, we turn your lists into a growth channel.

From welcome series and cart abandonment to lifecycle campaigns and promotional blasts, our strategies are built to increase engagement and revenue. Whether you need list growth, deliverability improvements, or full-funnel automation — we turn messages into measurable business outcomes.

![Email & SMS marketing](/images/opai-img-52.png)

Our Email & SMS marketing service combines best-in-class tools with data-driven strategy. We use advanced segmentation, A/B testing, and behavioral triggers to deliver the right message to the right subscriber at the right moment.

We help you grow your list, improve open and click rates, and tie every send to revenue. From identifying high-value segments to reducing unsubscribes and spam complaints, we ensure your email and SMS programs scale with your business.

### What we do

- Build and segment email and SMS lists for targeted campaigns
- Design automated flows (welcome, nurture, win-back, post-purchase)
- Run A/B tests on subject lines, copy, and send times
- Monitor deliverability and inbox placement
- Report on opens, clicks, conversions, and revenue
